Azzé Host

Play the music on your computer with Azzé on your phone, tablet or Chromebook: at home over Wi-Fi, and from anywhere with Azzé Premium.

Choose a download Free. No account needed on the computer.

Downloads

Windows

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit (ARM PCs too)

Azze-Setup.exe

Installs for you only, with no administrator needed. If Windows SmartScreen asks, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Chromebook

With Linux turned on

Intel or AMD ARM
  1. Turn on Linux: Settings › About ChromeOS › Developers › Linux development environment.
  2. Download the package for your Chromebook. This page picks the right one when you open it on the Chromebook.
  3. Open it in the Files app and choose Install. Azzé Host appears in the Launcher, under Linux apps.
  4. Let your devices reach it: in the same Linux settings, open Port forwarding and add TCP 47379, TCP 47380, UDP 47380 and TCP 47381. Then type the Chromebook's Wi-Fi address in Azzé Host's Settings.

Linux

64-bit Intel or AMD, and ARM

Debian, Ubuntu, Linux Mint, Pop!_OS and others that use .deb:

.deb for Intel or AMD .deb for ARM 
sudo apt install ./azze-host-amd64.deb

Any other Linux: unpack it anywhere and run it. Nothing else to install.

.tar.gz for Intel or AMD .tar.gz for ARM 
tar -xzf azze-host-linux-x64.tar.gz
./azze-host/bin/azze-host

Azzé for Android

The app that plays it: phones, tablets and Chromebooks

Azzé Host shares the music. Azzé plays it: open Settings › Library › Computers & Servers, then Scan QR Code, and scan the code Azzé Host shows in Pair a Device. With Azzé Premium, the computer's music plays away from home too.

Get it on Google Play

Choose What Each Device Gets

Every device you pair can have its own music. In Azzé Host, open Settings, find the device and choose Choose Music: keep All Music on, or turn on just the albums and tracks you want. The device picks up the new list by itself.

Azzé Host isn't available for Mac.
